How to use Threadboard

  • Add a note with a color button on the left. Type directly into it.
  • Move a note by its top edge; resize it from the lower right corner.
  • Tie notes together by clicking one pin, then another. Click a string to select it and press Delete to remove it.
  • Remove with the ✕ button on the left, then click any note or string. Press Esc when you are done. Each delete asks first, unless you tick "Don't ask me again".
  • Undo with ↶ at the top of the toolbar or Ctrl/⌘ + Z; redo with ↷ or Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + Z.
  • Pan by dragging empty board space. Use the zoom controls or Ctrl/⌘ + scroll to zoom.
  • Notes and strings save automatically in this browser.

Delete this note?
